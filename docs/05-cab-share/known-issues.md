# Cab Share — Known Issues (full)

1. **Poster disappears** — VIT sessions expire during the drive; riders see a stale "X.XX credits" badge — refresh button pulls live status.
2. **Duplicate posts** — accidental double tap creates two rows; the tab de-dupes by (owner, time, route) — still possible with edits.
3. **Riding without payment dispute** — no wallet transfer on-platform; settlement is personal; AmazeCC makes zero promise about payment disputes on campus cabs.
4. **Woman safety** — matching prefers same-gender peers by default when the poster sets it; documented in the sheet; overrideable with explicit tap and a warning.
5. **Off-campus drops** — unsupported; BPP/CP/Vellore routes only; dropway enforced in form.
