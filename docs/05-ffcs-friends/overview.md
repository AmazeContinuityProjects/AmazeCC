# FFCS Friends — Overview (walkthrough)

> **In one line:** pick courses knowing whose in-section buddy you are — before you finalize.

1. **Planner → Friends** — your pair list (each: handle, shared course count, busy overlap %)
2. **Add** — scan their QR or paste handle → they get a ping (no phone cadence; in-app only)
3. **Pairing review** — see exactly which sections you share — always
4. **Group common-free** — one person picks a room; grid shows when all group members free
5. **Revoke** — long-press their row → "Forget" → gone from your device, gone from theirs (grant deletion)

Not a social network — no feeds, no DMs, no presence indicator. Just scheduling sanity.
