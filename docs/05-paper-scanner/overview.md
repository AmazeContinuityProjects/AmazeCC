# Paper Scanner — Overview (walkthrough)

> **In one line:** photograph your exam paper, walk away with a clean, searchable, correctly-oriented PDF on your phone — no upload.

1. **Tools → Scanner** — camera opens full-screen with frame guides and torch toggle
2. **Capture** — edges snap-detected; tap shutter; auto-crop preview adjustable
3. **Review page** — enhancement filters (auto, grayscale, contrast); rotate until horizontal text reads right
4. **Scan another page** — multi-page PDF assembled in one go
5. **Save** — stored locally; optional "attach to course" tags it for the paper

Use case: compiling your own FAT bank from your semester's sheets — hours saved per semester.
