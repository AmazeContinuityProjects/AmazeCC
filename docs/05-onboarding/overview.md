# Onboarding — Overview (walkthrough)

> **In one line:** `&lt;2 minutes` from "never heard of AmazeCC" to a populated dashboard.

1. **Landing page** — what this is, campus brand hero, one line: "VIT's portal, rebuilt"
2. **Sign in** — VTOP credentials; captcha; sync begins immediately (cookie auth handshakes)
3. **Sync screen** — full-screen sync: attendance, marks, grades — with a cancellable progress bar
4. **Welcome** — your name, branch, reg — "you're in" → enter app
5. **Demo mode** — same tour, mock student — perfect for friends curious about your phone

Students who finish here have touched every sync path the app has.
