# QBank — Overview (walkthrough)

> **In one line:** every past paper VIT publishes, searchable, with model answers — one place, inside the app.

1. **Tools → QBank** — browse catalog; search by course code, topic, exam type (CAT1/CAT2/FAT)
2. **Paper card** — year, semester, exam type, marks distribution — tap to open
3. **Question view** — typed/written answers + step markers; math renders inline
4. **Practice mode** — pick a paper → hide answers → work → reveal — offline friendly
5. **Contribute** — upload your collected papers (requires VTOP verification) — moderated before release

No account linking to your results — it's a study tool, not a grade record.
