# Feedback & Support — Known Issues (full)

1. **Console noise leaks** — 30-line console tail may include reg-number strings embedded in warnings; regex should blank most shapes; report suspects via email, not public thread.
2. **Recording bar light stays on** — after first tap it pulses 60 s — students sometimes forget it's live; it auto-stops after 90 s.
3. **Replay doesn't capture touches on canvas-heavy tabs** — mirrored preview skips pixel-perfect canvas replays by design.
4. **Feedback spam** — vague "it broken" reports with no context aren't actionable — the app asks for one reproducer step; even "refresh fixes it" is useful.
5. **No language translation** — English-only feedback; international students described English issues in broken English every week — auto-translate could help someday; not built yet.
