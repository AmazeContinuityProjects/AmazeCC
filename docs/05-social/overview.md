# Social — Overview (walkthrough)

> **In one line:** see who's in your sections, pair up, form groups, find common free hours — without exposing your timetable until you opt in.

1. **More → Social** — your handle is your identity (share via QR or copy button)
2. **Add a peer**: scan their QR or paste their handle — they get a request
3. **Accept requests** — the pairing round trip happens automatically in seconds
4. **People list** — search, common-course count, degree completion estimate
5. **Classes Together** — your shared class list per peer
6. **Common Free Grid** — visual matrix: this week, every period, colored by who's free
7. **Groups** — buy lunch buddies, carve a study group, dedupe by busy overlap
8. **Pairs (revoke)** — before dropping-out of a pair, see what leaves your cache

Revocation note: deleting a peer association removes their timetable from your cache immediately
and asks the server to stop deriving for you both.
