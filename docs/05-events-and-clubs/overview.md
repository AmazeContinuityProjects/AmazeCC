# Events & Clubs — Overview (walkthrough)

> **In one line:** what's happening on campus, what you're eligible for, and how to register — from inside the app.

1. **Event Hub** — today/this week filters; badges for your degree/section matches; venue maps inline
2. **Register** — one tap; VTOP processes your seat; countdown to event starts
3. **Receipt** — download at end of event; PDF from EventHub (we don't forge these)
4. **My registered** — events you're in; linked to your attendance claim at checkout
5. **Club hub** — 100+ clubs directory; filter by interest; feed of announcements per club; join links

Everything VIT official than this tab is handled by VIT's portal — this is the easy front door.
